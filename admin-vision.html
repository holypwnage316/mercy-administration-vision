Administrative Proposal

A working place to turn administrative ideas and volunteer ministry needs into clear committee-driven workflows Mercy can implement, test, and hand off.

Lindsey's committee growth ideas offer one helpful starting point for discussing how administrative work and volunteer ministries can be organized through committees.

1

Purpose

🧩From vision to workflow

Administrative improvement only becomes useful when it turns into repeatable ministry practice. Lindsey's thoughts can help Mercy name the recurring moments that need a clear process, then decide who owns each step and which tools should support it.

At Mercy, committees are one of the main ways administrative work and volunteer ministry become organized action. A healthy committee process gives leaders clarity about vision, authority, oversight, and feedback without requiring every decision to restart from scratch.

The goal is to make important care and communication easier to repeat, easier to track, and less dependent on one person remembering every detail.
2

First workflow to implement

🏛️Committee growth workflow

Lindsey's committee workflow gives Mercy a repeatable way to move from officer vision to team action. It clarifies who appoints the committee, how the team is gathered, what the committee is free to do, how oversight works, and how growth is measured.

Adapted from Lindsey's Committee Growth Flowchart.

1

Committee is appointed

The officers appoint a committee chair. Specific vision and directives are given, including what needs approval and what can be decided and acted upon by the committee.

2

Gather a team

The team lead gathers members to serve on the committee, schedules periodic meetings, and communicates the vision and directives.

3

Action

The team executes the session's directives while also receiving feedback from the congregation. The team is released to act within its specific vision and purview.

4

Oversight

The team gathers information and creates suggestions for the officers according to its directives. The team lead communicates with the appointed overseer and receives further direction.

5

Growth

The team continues to grow in number and effectiveness through clear communication, vision, oversight, and teamwork.

4

Workflow map

🗺️Workflows to define

These are starting points. Each workflow can be connected back to a committee or team that owns the work, receives oversight, and communicates progress clearly.

First-time guest follow-up

Connection card, guest record, thank-you message, ministry introduction, next step.

Likely Tithely support: People, Forms, Messaging

Member care and birthdays

Important dates, reminders, assigned follow-up, pastoral or ministry touchpoints.

Likely Tithely support: People, Tags, Follow-ups, Automations

Volunteer onboarding

Interest form, ministry leader follow-up, role assignment, scheduling, reminders.

Likely Tithely support: People, Groups, Messaging, Service Planning

Ministry group communication

Group roster, leader messaging, announcements, reminders, replies in one place.

Likely Tithely support: Groups, Messaging, Church App

Weather and urgent updates

Clear decision point, approved message, targeted send list, fast delivery.

Likely Tithely support: Messaging, People, Groups

Worship service preparation

Service plan, worship roles, reminders, outline sharing, Sunday readiness.

Likely Tithely support: Service Planning, Messaging
5

Implementation pattern

🛠️How to build each workflow

Each workflow should be written in the same format so it can move from idea to practice without getting stuck as a general aspiration.

Need What ministry or administrative problem are we solving?
Trigger What starts the workflow? A form, a visit, a date, an event, or a request?
Owner Which committee, team lead, or ministry leader is responsible for the work?
Authority What can the committee decide, and what needs officer or session approval?
Steps What happens first, second, third, and afterward?
Oversight Who receives updates, gives direction, and helps remove blockers?
Tithely support Which Tithely tool helps: People, Messaging, Groups, Forms, or Service Planning?
Review rhythm How will Mercy know whether the workflow is working?
