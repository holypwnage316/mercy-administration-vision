Ministry Pathways and Administrative Care

A proposal for helping people at Mercy move more naturally from visiting, to belonging, to serving, to growing.

1

Summary

The big idea

Mercy has an opportunity to strengthen the way people are welcomed, followed up with, communicated with, and connected into the life of the church. Some of this work is in-person hospitality. Some of it is administrative structure. Together, they form a ministry pathway.

The goal is not to buy more tools. The goal is to build clearer ministry pathways, and then use the tools we already have or are considering to support those pathways.
2

Why it matters

The ministry problem this addresses

Guests, members, leaders, and volunteers often need different kinds of care and communication. Without a clear shared system, important follow-up can depend on memory, scattered messages, hallway conversations, or individual leaders carrying too much in their heads.

A clearer pathway would help Mercy become more consistent without becoming impersonal. The aim is not to automate care, but to make sure care is less likely to fall through the cracks.

3

Proposed improvements

What could be strengthened

Sunday morning hospitality

Create clearer roles for greeters and parking lot attendants. Greeters would welcome people, introduce themselves to first-time guests, and help people find restrooms, children’s areas, and hospitality. Parking lot attendants could direct cars and help people who need to be dropped off closer to the entrance.

Result: guests experience care before they have to ask for help.

First-time guest follow-up

Create a consistent guest process: a connection card is filled out, entered into the church database, and added to a first-time guest workflow. On Monday, the guest receives a thank-you email that explains Mercy’s ministries and invites a next step.

Result: guest care becomes consistent, warm, and repeatable.

Church people database

Use a people database to organize members, attenders, guests, volunteers, ministry leaders, birthdays, groups, and custom categories. This would make follow-up, pastoral care, and ministry coordination easier to track.

Result: leaders have a clearer shared picture of people and needs.

Messaging and group communication

Use groups for staff, ministry leaders, worship team, volunteers, small groups, and other ministry teams. Leaders could send reminders, encouragements, weather updates, and group-specific communication from one shared place.

Result: fewer scattered messages and clearer communication.

Worship and service planning

Use a service planning process to organize songs, sermon notes, readings, transitions, volunteers, and service outlines. Automatic reminders could help people know when and how they are serving.

Result: Sunday teams prepare with less friction and more clarity.

4

Platform connection

How Tithely and Subsplash fit

Tithely and Subsplash should be evaluated by how well they support Mercy’s ministry pathway, not just by how many features they list. The most useful features are the ones that make welcome, follow-up, communication, giving, groups, and service planning easier to sustain.

Welcome and guests Forms, people records, workflows, email and text follow-up.
Communication Email, text, push notifications, announcements, group messages.
Belonging Groups, group chat, small group discovery, volunteer teams.
Generosity Digital giving, recurring giving, donor records, statements, analytics.
Sunday worship Service planning, volunteer scheduling, reminders, media and livestream tools.
5

Suggested next steps

A reasonable starting point

  1. Agree on the ministry pathway language: welcome, follow-up, belonging, service, and growth.
  2. Start with the Sunday morning hospitality roles because they are visible and concrete.
  3. Create the first-time guest workflow as the first administrative system to test.
  4. Map each ministry need to the Tithely or Subsplash tools that would actually support it.
  5. Decide what should be implemented first, what can wait, and who needs to own each part.
